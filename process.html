HOW WE WORK

Every transaction starts
with understanding.

Our process is designed to bring structure to the conversations and decisions behind agricultural commodity trade.

Start a conversation
THE WORKFLOW

A clearer path
from enquiry onward.

Each potential opportunity is different. These are the core stages we use to guide an initial assessment and conversation.

01
STAGE 01

Define the requirement

Begin with the exact product, grade, volume, destination, timing and commercial expectations.

A better brief makes the next conversation more efficient.
02
STAGE 02

Assess information and counterparties

Review available supplier information, supporting product documents and claimed capabilities.

The scope and depth of checks depend on the opportunity.
03
STAGE 03

Coordinate commercial terms

Help the relevant parties discuss specifications, pricing, payment arrangements, logistics and responsibilities.

Terms only become binding through the parties' applicable agreements.
04
STAGE 04

Support transaction progression

Coordinate agreed next steps toward execution and delivery within the scope of the arrangement.

The roles and responsibilities of each party are defined for the particular transaction.
OUR WORKING PRINCIPLE

Clear expectations.
Better decisions.

We do not present tentative supply or indicative discussions as guaranteed transactions. The product, parties, payment structure and contractual obligations must be confirmed for each opportunity.

OUR TRADE MODEL

Introductions, brokerage or direct trade.

RunAgri can explore different commercial arrangements where appropriate. The role, scope and responsibilities are agreed on a transaction-by-transaction basis.
